About Me

Vhagar, known as the Ancient Fury, is one of the largest and most fearsome dragons in the world of House of the Dragon. Once ridden by Queen Visenya Targaryen during the conquest of the Seven Kingdoms alongside Balerion the Black Dread and Meraxes, Vhagar became a symbol of Targaryen power and dominance.

With her colossal size, devastating dragonfire, and centuries of battle experience, Vhagar is a formidable force capable of laying waste to armies and striking terror into her enemies. Though older and slower than she once was, her immense strength and ferocity make her one of the most dangerous dragons ever to soar across Westeros.

Fire made her legendary. Time made her a terror.
